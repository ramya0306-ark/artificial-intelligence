ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is one of the most important and rapidly growing technologies in the modern world.
It refers to the ability of machines or computer programs to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence.
These tasks include learning, reasoning, problem-solving, understanding language, and recognizing images or speech....

Did you know there are four distinct types of artificial intelligence?

1.Reactive Machines
2.Limited Memory
3.Theory of Mind
4.Self-Aware AI

Reactive Machines:
 These AI systems have no memory and act solely based on the current input.
They cannot learn from past experiences or anticipate future outcomes.
For example, a rule-based chatbot that provides the same response to the same query would be considered a reactive machine.

Limited Memory:
 This type of AI can store past data and use it to make decisions.
It can learn from its experiences, but its memory is finite, meaning
it can only recall a limited amount of past information.
Self-driving cars, for instance, can utilize limited memory AI by remembering past routes and driving conditions to optimize their navigation.

Theory of Mind:
This AI type is hypothetical and would involve the ability to understand and
predict the mental states of others, including their thoughts, feelings, and beliefs.
This is a significant step towards human-like intelligence, as it involves understanding the intentions and motivations of others.

Self-Aware AI:
 This is the most advanced and also theoretical type of AI.
It would involve the ability to have consciousness, self-awareness, and a sense of self.
Self-aware AI would not only understand its environment and interact intelligently but also have an understanding of itself as an entity
